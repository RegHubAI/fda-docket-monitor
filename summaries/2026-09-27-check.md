## Watch-list resolution (predicted surge confirmed)

The Jul 28 note expected an Aug 17–24 trade-group surge. It happened:

- **PhRMA (-0191):** supports FIH acceleration; skeptical QRI scope ("currently unclear how the proposed use of QRIs will accelerate" FIH); wants published certification criteria, non-binding QRI advice, CMC decisions kept with FDA, rolling submission+review as "the most impactful element," voluntary broad eligibility, COI and confidentiality safeguards, no premium pathway.
- **BIO (-0150):** supports goal, questions QRI layer vs. "direct, FDA-led approaches"; recommends rolling review **decoupled** from QRI review and a public workshop; equity concerns (fee-for-service → "de facto premium pathway"; co-application excludes small sponsors); process-only — not evidentiary — differences for expedited INDs.
- **QRI-eligibility land rush:** CROs (IQVIA, Labcorp ×2, Parexel, ICON, PPD/Thermo Fisher, WCG, Advarra ×2, Veristat, Altasciences, Precision for Medicine, Allucent) and AMCs (MD Anderson, City of Hope, Cleveland Clinic, Duke, MSK, Baylor, Seattle Children's, UC Davis) all filed — clear positioning for QRI designation.
- **Incubate (-0204)** filed, as flagged in July from its House testimony.
- Patient/disease groups well represented: Friends of Cancer Research, ALS Association, CF Foundation, Alzheimer's Association, Breakthrough T1D, GO2 for Lung Cancer, 1Day Sooner, MRCT Center.
